Available variables

Use variables with the # prefix:

Example:

#basePrice * #nights * #roomsCount * 0.90

Example with a returning-customer adjustment:

(#basePrice * #nights * #roomsCount) - (#previousReservations * 5)
The formula is checked before it is saved. If the hotel has no formula, or a stored formula cannot be calculated for a reservation, the standard price is used.
